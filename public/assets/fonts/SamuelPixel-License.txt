SamuelPixel.ttf

Derived from Kenney Pixel (KenneyPixel.ttf, Kenney, www.kenney.nl, License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by tools/make_font.py. Glyph shapes are re-rasterised from
Kenney Pixel on a 16px grid with some glyphs (l, i, j, I, 1, |, t, 0, 8) redrawn for readability.
Advance widths are identical to Kenney Pixel except the space (2.5px -> 3px, so words land on whole pixels); vertical metrics are identical.

This derived font is released under CC0 as well: free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects.
